Heat Check

Flagged 10 of 97 buildings for review, worth about $159K and 576 t CO2 a year.

Finds which buildings waste heat and what to fix first. Per-building models with calibrated 90% ranges, an ASHRAE accuracy gate, and a "can't judge" verdict instead of guessing. Built solo in one day at Hackachusetts 2026; deployed with Docker and CI/CD.

ML · Energy · Telecom

Project Zero-Watt

Cut about 25% of daily tower energy in simulation, worth $2.7M to $4.8M a year across a national network.

5G towers burn power around the clock, even when traffic is near zero. An XGBoost model forecasts traffic an hour ahead with 1.9% error and puts idle towers into deep sleep. A safety rule only allows sleep after three low readings in a row, and a 100-run stress test measured the risk of a wrong call at about 3%. Built on a physics-based simulation of tower power.

Data: Simulated 5G tower telemetry from a physics-based model of radio power use, with added sensor noise.

Analytics · Product · Go-to-market

TaskRabbit Select

Raised my hourly rate 25% with no drop in client satisfaction, then turned the finding into a premium product and launch plan.

I analyzed 484 of my own TaskRabbit jobs across nearly four years to test whether clients pay for trust rather than price. Category, location, and timing predicted premium demand, and higher rates showed no link to lower tips. That led to "Select," a premium matching tier for top Taskers, with a go-to-market plan built around an NYC weekend pilot.

Data: My own TaskRabbit job, rate, and review records, 2018 to 2025.

Master's CapstoneML · Customer AI

IntentCX: AI agent routing

Gradient Boosting reached 0.81 AUC (0.83 cross-validated) predicting which of 8,000 customers would convert.

Routes high-intent customers to premium AI agents and low-intent ones to a lower-cost path, cutting wasted spend and AI compute. Behavior drove the score; demographics added almost nothing, so routing can leave out sensitive attributes. Compared regression, classification, and clustering, and reported the null clustering result honestly. Boston University.

Data: Public Kaggle digital marketing dataset, 8,000 customers. No collection method is documented and it appears synthetic, which the report addresses.

Winner, Sustainability Smart waste · Campus operations

Bin There Done That

Found where BU's BigBelly bins overflow and where they sit empty, and showed how to move bins and cut pickups.

Mapped BigBelly smart bin fill data along the Commonwealth Ave corridor to find hotspots and coldspots, comparing BU-owned and City of Boston bins. Recommended moving bins from cold to hot areas, optimizing pickup routes, and acquiring city bins in hotspots, in support of BU's goal to divert 90% of waste from landfill by 2030. Five-person team from BU, Northeastern, and Bentley; won the sustainability category at Civic Hacks.

Data: BigBelly smart bin fill data for BU and City of Boston bins along Commonwealth Ave.

Responsible AI · Policy

Agentic AI and Labor Displacement

Proposes four enforceable rules for companies whose AI agents replace workers at scale.

Klarna's AI assistant did the work of 700 employees in one month, with no notice or transition support required. The policy framework calls for advance notice to workers, public impact assessments, retraining funds paid from automation savings, and shared accountability for model providers. Responsible and Ethical Data Science, Boston University.

Data: Klarna's public statements plus 14 sources on labor economics, AI governance, and policy precedent.
